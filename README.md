## Overview

**DaVinciA+™** (*Deterministic & Auditable Verification Infrastructure for Constitutional Intelligence & Autonomy*) defines a machine-enforceable architectural boundary separating **probabilistic model capability** from **institutional state-mutation authority**.

Under `DaVinciA+ v1.0`, autonomous software agents and generative models hold **zero direct write privileges** over authoritative state (`AGENT_DIRECT_MUTATION_PROHIBITED`). Every human gesture, voice command, natural-language prompt, and agent proposal compiles into a schema-validated **Typed Action Envelope** (`davincia.action.v1`), clears fail-closed **Policy & EU AI Act Article 14 Human-Authority Gates**, executes solely through a deterministic **State Reducer**, and emits a hash-chained **Cryptographic Decision Receipt** (`davincia.receipt.v1`).
